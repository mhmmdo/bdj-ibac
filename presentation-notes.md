# Presentation Notes — Purun Puan: Digital Business Strategy Pitch

**Presentation Duration:** 8–10 Minutes  
**Target Audience:** Venture Capitalists, Digital Transformation Judges, and Business Strategists  
**Objective:** Present the digital conversion strategy, brand positioning, and B2C/B2B business value pipeline that digitalizes Purun Puan from a local commodity into a high-margin global brand.

---

## 00:00 - 01:15 | Brand Positioning & Shifting the Value Curve (The Hook)
*   **Visual Focus:** Hero Section (Premium Walnut-on-Linen typography, clean layout)
*   **Presenter Pitch (Business Strategy & Brand Positioning):**
    > "Good morning, judges. Today, we are pitching the digital business model of Purun Puan. 
    > 
    > When local crafts try to enter global markets, they usually fail because they compete on price as a low-margin commodity. Our strategy shifts the value curve. 
    > 
    > We are positioning Purun Puan as a premium, culturally rooted lifestyle brand. We target the lucrative global LOHAS (Lifestyles of Health and Sustainability) consumer segment. These buyers do not buy products; they buy carbon-conscious stories and fair-trade ethics.
    > 
    > Through digital storytelling and structured transparency, we justify a 300% price premium over local commodity prices, transforming a local craft into a highly profitable, scalable global business."

---

## 01:15 - 02:30 | Supply Chain & Meet the Artisans (Section C)
*   **Visual Focus:** Craft Section & "Meet the Artisans" Profile Gallery
*   **Presenter Pitch (Social Impact & Humanizing the Brand):**
    > "Our operational model is built on fair-trade decentralization, pooling the skills of rural female weavers, or 'Puan,' into craft circles.
    > 
    > To connect global buyers with our makers, we built an interactive **Artisan Profile Gallery** featuring the weavers behind the collections, like Ibu Salamah and Ibu Hamdanah.
    > 
    > Visitors can hover over their profiles to read personal quotes about how weaving purun pays for their grandchildren's education and keeps local forests wet. By humanizing the supply chain, we build deep emotional trust, transforming ethical transparency into brand loyalty."

---

## 02:30 - 03:45 | Material Innovation & Weave Texture Selector (Section B)
*   **Visual Focus:** Material Section & "Interactive Weave Showcase" Block
*   **Presenter Pitch (UX Taction & Product Education):**
    > "Because natural crafts are highly tactile, buying them online presents a challenge: buyers cannot touch the product. We solve this with the **Interactive Weave Showcase**.
    > 
    > Visitors can click through different weaving techniques—Raw Natural Weave, Fine Diagonal Weave, or Sasirangan Fabric Lining—to instantly swap and inspect zoomed high-resolution texture details.
    > 
    > This interactive tool educates the buyer on the complexity of handweaving, highlights the integration of traditional Banjarmasin Sasirangan cotton, and recreates the tactile showroom experience digitally."

---

## 03:45 - 05:00 | Conversational Commerce & The Matchmaker Quiz (Section D & Quiz)
*   **Visual Focus:** Collection Grid & the "Find Your Match" Interactive Quiz
*   **Presenter Pitch (Conversational CRO & Bounce Rate Reduction):**
    > "Our catalog showcases 6 premium products, from totes to utility backpacks. However, in e-commerce, offering options often leads to choice paralysis, which increases bounce rates.
    > 
    > To optimize our conversion funnel, we built a conversational sales agent: the **Find Your Match Quiz**.
    > 
    > Based on e-commerce psychology, the quiz asks 3 lifestyle questions to match products with the user's daily habits and values.
    > 
    > This interactive quiz acts as a conversational CRO (Conversion Rate Optimization) tool. It engages the visitor, filters the catalog dynamically, and recommends a single, personalized match. This reduces decision friction, increasing our conversion rate by matching user intent with the right product instantly."

---

## 05:00 - 06:15 | The Signature Feature: The Craft Passport
*   **Visual Focus:** The Craft Passport Drawer (Open state, showcasing maker, care, and impact details)
*   **Presenter Pitch (Trust Engine & Digital Authentication):**
    > "In global trade, the biggest barrier to high-value purchases is information asymmetry. How does a buyer in Tokyo or New York verify that a bag is truly hand-woven, eco-friendly, and ethically produced?
    > 
    > We solve this through our digital signature feature: the **Craft Passport**.
    > 
    > When a buyer views a product, they access a detailed digital provenance profile. It verifies the exact weaver (Maker), the specific wetland swamp (Origin), the raw material processing (Process), and the ecological contribution (Impact).
    > 
    > This passport functions as a digital trust engine. It removes buyer hesitation, reduces cart abandonment, and justifies a premium price point by providing verifiable provenance in a single click."

---

## 06:15 - 07:30 | B2C Shopping Cart, WhatsApp Checkout, & Floating Widget
*   **Visual Focus:** Restored Shopping Bag drawer with live subtotal adjustments, and Floating WhatsApp widget
*   **Presenter Pitch (Frictionless Checkout & High-Trust Commerce):**
    > "Let us look at the purchase transaction flow. Setting up complex international credit card merchant gateways can be a major technical and financial barrier for emerging local brands.
    > 
    > Our system uses a hybrid model: the **WhatsApp Checkout Bridge**. The user manages their items in a modern, standard shopping cart drawer that calculates subtotals dynamically. 
    > 
    > But when they click 'Proceed to Checkout', we redirect them to WhatsApp with a pre-formatted, bilingual invoice listing all items, quantities, and prices.
    > 
    > We also integrated a **Floating WhatsApp Widget** at the bottom-right corner, prompting instant customer support to capture leads before they bounce.
    > 
    > This bridges B2C cart convenience with conversational checkout behavior. It resolves shipping calculation friction (since shipping rates for hand-woven items are highly variable) and allows the business to offer high-trust, personalized payment links, ensuring a much higher final transaction completion rate."

---

## 07:30 - 08:30 | Ecological Asset & Impact Calculator (Section A & Cart Calculator)
*   **Visual Focus:** Origin Section (Peatlands) & Cart Drawer Impact Badge
*   **Presenter Pitch (Sustainability Value & Resource Management):**
    > "Let us look at our raw material asset. The peatlands of South Kalimantan are critical carbon sinks. Purun Puan introduces a circular economy model: wild purun grass grows naturally without draining the swamps. By harvesting it, we protect the wetlands.
    > 
    > To make this environmental value verifiable, we integrated a **Peatland Impact Calculator** directly into the Shopping Bag drawer. 
    > 
    > As a buyer adds items to their cart, the system dynamically calculates their real-world contribution. For example, buying 2 bags is shown to preserve 3.0 square meters of peatlands and prevent 1.6 kilograms of CO₂ emissions. 
    > 
    > This translates abstract carbon values into a tangible, personalized ecological contribution, boosting conversion among green consumers."

---

*   **Visual Focus:** Digital Catalog Banner, Timeline, and Press Section
*   **Presenter Pitch (B2B Client Acquisition & Social Proof):**

We display key credentials—such as being a Kemenparekraf AKI Finalist, participating in Bank Indonesia's Tokyo Showcase, and ranking in the Top 67 Gernas BBI out of 1,178 businesses. This builds the brand authority necessary to close high-value B2B contracts."

## 09:30 - 10:00 | Technical Efficiency & Conclusion
*   **Visual Focus:** Language Toggle (EN/ID) & Responsive Layout

## 💡 Q&A Guide: Defending the Ecological Calculator Numbers

If a judge asks: **"What is the scientific basis for the 1.5 m² and 0.8 kg CO₂ figures?"**
Here is your easy English answer:

1. **For the 1.5 square meters:**
   * *"One standard Purun bag requires around 150 to 200 stalks of grass."*
   * *"In Palam wetlands, this amount grows naturally in about 1 to 1.5 square meters."*

2. **For the 0.8 kilograms of carbon:**
   * *"Indonesia's peatlands store 10 times more carbon than tropical rainforests."*
   * *"If they are drained or burned for palm oil, they release carbon."*
   * *"Our number is a conservative estimate of the carbon emissions saved by keeping the peatland wet and harvesting wild grass instead of draining the swamp."*
