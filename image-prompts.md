# Image Generation Prompts — Purun Puan

These prompts are designed to maintain a consistent, warm, tactile, and premium editorial aesthetic following the **HOUSEPLANT** style reference. 

When generating, prioritize natural light, shallow depth of field, warm earth tones (terracotta, olive green, warm wood, raw cream), and realistic textures. Avoid generic studio backdrops or overly saturated colors.

---

## 1. Hero Background / Peatland Context
- **Prompt:** `An editorial wide photo of the wetlands of South Kalimantan, Indonesia, during sunrise. Tall, slender green purun sedge grass (Lepironia articulata) growing out of shallow, clear wetland swamp water. Earthy, mist rising, soft golden sunlight, high detail, warm organic tones, cinematic lighting, shot on 35mm lens. --ar 16:9 --style raw`
- **Use Case:** Hero header background or the introduction background for the "Origin" section.

## 2. Artisan Hands Weaving (Process Context)
- **Prompt:** `A close-up photographic shot of a South Kalimantan woman's hands weaving dried, flattened purun grass reeds into an intricate pattern. Focus is on the detailed, textured reeds and the natural movement of the hands. Warm natural sunbeams streaming through a window, wooden table background, tactile quality, calm and organic feel, shallow depth of field, high texture detail. --ar 4:3 --style raw`
- **Use Case:** "The Craft" section or the background of the process explanation.

## 3. Product 1: Savara Woven Tote

The bag has premium dark brown leather handles and small brass rivets. Set on a rustic wooden bench next to a folded linen cloth, natural soft side-lighting, warm earth-tone shadows. --ar 1:1 --style raw`
